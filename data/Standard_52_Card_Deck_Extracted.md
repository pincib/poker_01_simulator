# Standard 52-Card Deck: Complete Extracted Dataset
*Extracted directly from the official [Standard 52-card deck - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_52-card_deck) article.*

## Overview
The standard English-pattern 52-card pack consists of **13 ranks** in each of the **4 French suits**: **Clubs (♣)**, **Diamonds (♦)**, **Hearts (♥)**, and **Spades (♠)**.

Below is the complete catalog of all 52 cards, including standard poker notation, Unicode codepoints, Wikipedia file links, traditional & 4-colour deck colors, and historical nicknames.
